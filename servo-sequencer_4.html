How the buttons work

  • Double-click a servo's name anywhere on the page to rename it.
  • Open (D3) and Close (D4) move the servos to the poses set on this page.
  • Sequential off: all servos move at the same time.
  • Sequential on: servos move one at a time, in channel order (0 → 1 → 2 → 3), for both Open and Close. Timing slider: middle = each starts right as the previous one finishes · right = pause after (up to 4 s) · left = start early so they overlap (up to 4 s before).
  • Pressing the other button mid-move simply turns everything around.
  • Everything saves to the Arduino as you change it, so the buttons work with no PC attached.
  • At power-up the servos stay limp until the first button press, and that first move goes at full speed (the Arduino can't know where they were).
  • The Arduino's orange L LED is lit while servos are moving.
